About the Chamber

Building a stronger business community

Shaggar Chamber of Commerce & Sectoral Association gives businesses across every sector a platform to connect, exchange knowledge, discover markets and develop partnerships.

Through networking, information sharing, training, market connections, advocacy and collaboration, the Chamber works toward a more productive and connected business environment.

Our mission

To connect businesses, entrepreneurs and institutions with information, services, markets and partnerships that support sustainable growth.

Our vision

A dynamic business environment where enterprises connect, grow, access markets and take part in sustainable economic development.
